3. Recordings on the server

Open this page on your PC to download recordings. Storage is temporary: download soon after recording. A server restart or redeploy deletes everything.

Notes

  • Keep this page open and the screen on while recording. Phones pause or stop the camera and sensors when the page is in the background.
  • Timestamps use the phone’s performance.now(). Frame times are capture estimates. Motion events carry browser dispatch times, not hardware sensor timestamps.
  • Rotation rate is in degrees per second, as the browser reports it. Missing values are saved as null and shown as “unavailable”, never as 0.
  • The free server sleeps after about 15 minutes without traffic; the first load can take up to a minute.
